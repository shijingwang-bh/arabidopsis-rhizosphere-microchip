Figure S6. Gravity effects on root-exudate profiles of uninoculated Arabidopsis thaliana on days 3 and 7. Each point represents one LC–MS feature; G₃ and G₇ are the respective simulated-microgravity-minus-1-g effects. Blue points mark three features with temporal changes in the gravity effect (ΔG = G₇ − G₃); the dashed line marks equal effects. Responsive features had BH q < 0.05 and |effect| > log₂(1.5). Each gravity-by-day group comprised five independent collections.
