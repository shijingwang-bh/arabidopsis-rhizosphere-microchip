Figure S7. Day-3 inoculation responses of two Arabidopsis thaliana root-exudate metabolites. (a) 9,10-Dihydroxy-12,13-epoxyoctadecanoic acid (neg_5318). (b) 9,12-Octadecadiynoic acid (pos_12437). Each point represents an independent culture-dish collection (n = 5 per group); black lines and error bars show means and 95% confidence intervals on the log₂(intensity + 1) scale. Blue/orange denotes stationary 1 g/simulated microgravity (sµg). U/I, uninoculated/inoculated with Bacillus subtilis. Brackets show BH q for I versus U within each gravity condition; subtitles show BH q for the gravity-by-inoculation interaction.
