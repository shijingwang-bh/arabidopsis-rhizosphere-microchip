Figure 2. Arabidopsis thaliana responses after 7 days of gravity pre-exposure. (a) Lateral-root number under stationary 1 g (n = 12) and simulated microgravity (sµg, n = 14), shown as means ± SE with individual plants. The bracket gives the BH-adjusted P value from a two-sided t test. (b) Log₂ fold changes for eight differentially expressed leaf genes (BH q < 0.05), for sµg minus 1 g. (c) PCA of five 1-g and four sµg leaf pools, with five chips per pool and one point per pool. (d) Eight selected GO Biological Process sets. Position and point size indicate fgsea normalized enrichment score and gene-set size; CAMERA and directional FRY q values are shown. Blue/orange indicates 1 g/sµg in a,c and negative/positive effects in b,d.
