Figure 3. Bacterial accumulation in chip co-culture and growth in recovered plant-conditioned medium. (a) Merged images of GFP-expressing Arabidopsis thaliana roots (green) and RFP-labelled Bacillus subtilis (red) on day 3. Scale bars, 100 µm. (b) Daily root-zone RFP intensity on the same three chips per gravity condition. (c) Channel-liquid bacterial concentrations; points represent independent chips (1 g/sµg n = 4/5 on day 3 and 4/4 on day 7). Brackets give BH-adjusted P values from two-sided t tests. (d,e) Growth in filtered medium recovered from uninoculated plants on days 3 (d) and 7 (e), measured as background-corrected OD₆₀₀. Each gravity-by-day condition yielded one pooled preparation divided among technical wells. Well counts for 1 g-derived medium, sµg-derived medium and inoculated MS were 4/4/5 (d) and 5/4/5 (e). Data are means ± SE. Blue/orange denotes stationary 1 g/sµg; in d,e, these refer to the donor plants. Grey denotes inoculated half-strength MS. sµg, simulated microgravity.
