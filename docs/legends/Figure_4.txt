Figure 4. Root-exudate profiles, inoculation responses and temporal interactions in the independent culture-dish experiment. Arabidopsis thaliana was profiled on days 3 and 7 after Bacillus subtilis or mock inoculation under stationary 1 g or simulated microgravity (sµg). Groups comprised five independent collections, except day-7 inoculated sµg (n = 6). (a) PCA. (b) Conditional partial R² of gravity, inoculation, day and their interactions, with BH-adjusted permutation P values. (c) Inoculation effects for 579 features responsive in at least one of four inoculation contrasts; colours show mean log₂(intensity + 1) in inoculated samples minus that in uninoculated samples. Cluster labels give feature counts. (d) Day-3 and day-7 gravity-by-inoculation interactions (J₃, J₇); J is the inoculation effect under sµg minus that under 1 g. Coloured points mark temporal changes (T = J₇ − J₃); the dashed line marks J₃ = J₇. Responsive features had BH q < 0.05 and |effect| > log₂(1.5). U/I, uninoculated/inoculated; D3/D7, days 3/7.
