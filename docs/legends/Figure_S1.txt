Figure S1. Microchip preparation, preliminary imaging and cultivation assessment. (a) Manual loading of RFP-labelled Bacillus subtilis: internal panels A and C show fluorescence immediately after introduction and after 5 min; B shows the root and micropillar channel in bright field. Scale bars, 100 µm. (b) Syringe-pump connection used for loading. (c) Seedling preparation and chip placement. (d) Root length, leaf area and leaf fresh weight in the stationary 1-g cultivation comparison, measured 11 days after chip transfer. Non-chip control/chip n = 10/10, 5/6 and 12/12 plants, respectively. Data are means ± SE with individual plants; brackets give BH-adjusted P values from two-sided t tests.
