Figure 5. Day-7 root-exudate responses and growth of Arabidopsis thaliana after Bacillus subtilis inoculation. (a) Gravity-by-inoculation interaction (J₇) for LC–MS features in culture-dish collections. J₇ is the inoculation effect under simulated microgravity (sµg) minus that under stationary 1 g. Coloured points meet BH q < 0.05 and |J₇| > log₂(1.5); dashed lines mark these thresholds. Black rings identify the examples in b,c. (b,c) Isorhamnetin glycoside and sinapoyl glucose, shown as means with 95% confidence intervals and individual collections (n = 5 per group, except inoculated sµg, n = 6). (d,e) Mature-root diameter and leaf fresh weight after 7 days in chips, shown as means ± SE with individual plants. Group sizes for uninoculated 1 g, uninoculated sµg, inoculated 1 g and inoculated sµg were 3/3/5/4 (d) and 10/3/5/4 (e). Brackets give BH q values for the inoculation effect averaged equally across gravity conditions (linear model), corrected jointly across all four growth traits. Blue/orange in b–e denotes 1 g/sµg. U/I, uninoculated/inoculated.
