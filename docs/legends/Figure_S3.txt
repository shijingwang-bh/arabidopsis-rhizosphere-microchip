Figure S3. Gene-set responses in Arabidopsis thaliana leaves before bacterial inoculation. (a) Eight selected GO Biological Process sets. Points show fgsea normalized enrichment scores and their size denotes gene-set size; orange and blue indicate positive and negative enrichment for simulated microgravity (sµg) relative to stationary 1 g. CAMERA and directional FRY q values are shown. (b) Thirty-two representative leading-edge genes from four functional themes. Colours show row-standardized voom logCPM; adjacent columns show log₂ fold change and gene-level BH q. A1–A5/B1–B4 denote 1-g/sµg leaf pools.
