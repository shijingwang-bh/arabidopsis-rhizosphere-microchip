Figure S5a. Individual background-corrected OD₆₀₀ curves of Bacillus subtilis in conditioned-medium assays. Columns show medium recovered from uninoculated Arabidopsis thaliana chips under stationary 1 g or simulated microgravity (sµg), half-strength MS and LB; rows show day-3 and day-7 collections. Each curve represents one technical well; each gravity-by-day conditioned-medium group came from one pooled preparation. Blue curves show inoculated wells. The magenta dashed curve is the uninoculated day-7 E2 well, excluded from inoculated-well summaries. Panels use independent y-axis ranges.
